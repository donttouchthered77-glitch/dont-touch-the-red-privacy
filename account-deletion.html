Account and Data Deletion

App: Don't Touch the Red

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your Don't Touch the Red account and associated data, including accounts created anonymously when you first use the game.

How to request deletion

  1. Contact the developer at donttouchthered77@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line “Don't Touch the Red — Account and Data Deletion Request”.
  3. Include your in-game username. If you know your account ID, include it too. Do not send your password or other sensitive information.
  4. The developer may ask for reasonable information to identify the account and prevent deletion of the wrong account.

If you cannot access the game or do not know your account ID, you can still submit a request using your in-game username and any details that help identify your profile.

What data may be deleted

After the account is identified and the request is verified, the developer will delete the account and associated game data that can be linked to it, where applicable, such as:

  • the anonymous account identifier and account profile;
  • the in-game username;
  • leaderboard scores, gameplay statistics, and achievements associated with the account.

Data that may be retained

Some limited information may need to be retained where required by law, to resolve disputes, prevent fraud or abuse, or maintain security. If any data must be retained, it will be limited to what is necessary for that purpose and kept only for the applicable retention period. Data remaining in routine backups may persist until those backups are overwritten in accordance with the system's normal backup cycle.

Processing of requests

The developer will review the request, contact you if additional information is needed, and process verified requests as soon as reasonably practicable. Deletion of an account may be irreversible, and associated scores or statistics may not be recoverable.

Advertising and purchases

Deleting your game account does not necessarily delete records held independently by Google Play or advertising providers under their own services and policies. For data held by those providers, use the privacy or account controls offered by the relevant provider.

Contact

For account deletion requests or questions about this process, email donttouchthered77@gmail.com.
