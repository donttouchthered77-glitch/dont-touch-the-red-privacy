Privacy Policy — Don't Touch the Red

Last updated: October 14, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Don't Touch the Red ("the Game"), developed by the developer identified on its Google Play listing, handles information when you use the Game.

The Game is a reflex, attention, and memory game intended primarily for teenagers and adults.

2. Offline and Online Gameplay

An internet connection is required the first time you launch the Game to create an anonymous account for online features. After this initial setup, the Game can be played offline.

When an internet connection becomes available, eligible gameplay results stored locally may be synchronized with online services. Leaderboards and other online features require an internet connection.

3. Information We Process

Depending on the features you use, the following information may be processed:

  • Anonymous account information: an anonymous identifier used to support authentication and online features.
  • Username: the username you choose for the leaderboard.
  • Gameplay statistics: scores, best scores, total scores, games played, averages, and related leaderboard information.
  • Purchase information: the status of a purchase of the optional Remove Ads product. Payment processing is handled by Google Play.
  • Advertising and technical information: advertising providers may process device identifiers, IP address, diagnostics, and advertising interaction data, depending on their configuration and applicable consent requirements.

Certain gameplay progress, statistics, and preferences may also be stored locally on your device.

4. How Information Is Used

Information may be processed to:

  • Create and maintain anonymous accounts.
  • Save and synchronize online scores and leaderboard statistics.
  • Display and measure advertisements.
  • Verify purchases and apply the Remove Ads benefit.
  • Maintain, secure, and troubleshoot the Game.

5. Third-Party Services

The Game uses third-party services to provide certain features:

  • Supabase: anonymous authentication and online storage of player profiles and gameplay statistics. Supabase Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing: processing and verification of in-app purchases.
  • Google Mobile Ads / AdMob: serving interstitial advertisements and optional rewarded advertisements. Google Privacy Policy.

These providers may process information under their own privacy policies and terms. Their processing practices may change over time.

6. Data Sharing and Public Leaderboards

Your selected username and leaderboard scores may be visible to other players. Do not use personal or sensitive information as your username.

Information may be processed by the service providers described above as necessary to operate the Game. The developer does not sell personal information.

7. Data Retention and Deletion Requests

Some information is stored locally on your device. Online account and leaderboard information may remain stored for as long as necessary to operate the Game and its online features.

To request deletion of your online player profile and associated data, contact the developer using the contact email provided on the Game's Google Play listing. Include your in-game username so the request can be identified. Additional information may be requested when necessary to locate the relevant account.

Uninstalling the Game may remove locally stored information, but it does not necessarily delete information already synchronized with online services.

8. Audience and Children's Privacy

The Game is intended primarily for teenagers and adults and is not specifically designed or marketed for children. The Game does not intentionally request a child's real name, home address, or phone number for gameplay.

The Game uses third-party services, including advertising services. Their data practices depend on the applicable settings, consent requirements, and laws. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children in violation of applicable law.

If you believe that personal information has been processed inappropriately, contact the developer using the email provided on the Google Play listing.

9. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information processed through the Game. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Game, its features, or its data practices change. The latest version will be published at this page, with the updated date shown above.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the developer using the developer contact email shown on the Don't Touch the Red Google Play listing.
